# 08. Generative AI on Vertex

## Overview
Generative AI on Vertex AI provides access to Google's most advanced foundation models, including Gemini, for building AI-powered applications.

## Key Features
- **Model Garden:** A collection of foundation models for various modalities.
- **Generative AI Studio:** A console tool for quickly prototyping prompts.
- **Vertex AI SDK for Python:** Integrate Gemini into your applications.
- **Tuning and Distillation:** Adapt foundation models to your specific tasks.

## Enterprise Features
- **Safety Filters:** Ensure responsible AI usage.
- **Grounding:** Connecting models to your own data sources.
